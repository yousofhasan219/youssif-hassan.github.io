Data Quality Dashboard

Can the sales figures in this dataset be trusted before building any report on them?

6.18Mto4.22M

Total sales before and after removing duplicates: the original figure was overstated by 47%.

  • Reviewed 1,194 sales records from 2020 to 2025.
  • 390 duplicate rows, and 57% of rows share an Order ID.
  • No empty values or wrong dates, and 2025 sales stop at March 15.
Recommendation: validate Order IDs at data entry and remove duplicates before any report, otherwise revenue looks about 47% higher than reality.

Sales Profitability Dashboard

Do discounts really increase order volume, or do they just eat the profit?

14.3%to-2.2%

Average margin before and after discounts.

  • An average discount of 15.2% pushed margin from 14.3% down to -2.2%.
  • Discounts of 20 to 30% took margin to about -14%, versus about +11% for small discounts, without raising order volume.
  • Clothing leads sales (1.31M), Furniture has the highest margin (14.6%), and the North region leads sales.
Recommendation: cap discounts at 15% to protect profit margin.

Excel PivotTables, 1,000 sales | Public dataset | 2023
